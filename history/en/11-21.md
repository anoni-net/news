## 2026 {#y2026}

The Facebook and Google websites you use outside mainland China do not open from networks inside it (according to the third-party measurement site GreatFire, the last 61 conclusive tests of facebook.com and the last 13 of google.com all failed; we checked on 15 November 2026). The first World Internet Conference opened in Wuzhen, Zhejiang province, on 19 November 2014, and AFP reported, citing attendees, that participants had unfettered access during the conference to sites such as Facebook and Twitter that were unavailable in the rest of China (China Digital Times, a news site that follows internet censorship in China, specified that the access was at the conference location). A Bloomberg report excerpted by China Digital Times said that China often lifts its controls on the web for attendees of high-profile international forums, and that earlier the same month the internet service in the media centre at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) meeting in Beijing had also allowed access to Facebook and Twitter. The conference ran until 21 November, and a TIME report the following year said that once it ended, the Great Firewall, China's system for filtering foreign websites, came down again. We suggest that readers travelling to mainland China look up the addresses of the sites they use on GreatFire before they leave, since its records are measurements of connections made from networks inside the country.
