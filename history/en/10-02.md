## 2026 {#y2026}

Which flaws your Android phone has fixed depends on the Android security update date in its settings, and the older that date, the more flaws remain open. On 2 October 2023, Google's Android Security Bulletin listed a flaw in the driver for Arm Mali, a graphics chip used in phones, noting indications that it may be under limited, targeted exploitation. Help Net Security reported that Mali chips are used in Android phones from Google, Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo and other makers. Attacks aimed at a small number of people like this one target the same kind of people as the Apple threat notifications in our story the same day (see [Recognising and responding to Apple threat notifications](https://anoni.net/news/en/2026/10/apple-threat-notifications/)). To check your phone, open Settings, then About phone and Android version, and look at the Android security update date, then look for new updates under System and Software updates.
