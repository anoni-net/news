## 2026 {#y2026}

On 30 September 2021, the DST Root CA X3 cross-sign expired, and Let's Encrypt began relying on its own root, ISRG Root X1, for trust. A device trusts an HTTPS certificate only if it has the root that issued it built in. Old devices that no longer receive system updates cannot get the new root, and Let's Encrypt's explainer named the iPhone 4 and the HTC Dream as examples. Older Android versions got three more years from a special cross-sign that ran past the root's own expiry, and that certificate expired on 30 September 2024, with Let's Encrypt warning users of Android 7.0 and earlier that they might need to act. In November 2025 Let's Encrypt announced its next "Generation Y" roots, again cross-signed from the old ones so the new roots have time to reach devices.
