## 2026 {#y2026}

As of 1 October, people whose phones are stuck on Android 7 or earlier see certificate warnings on websites that use Let's Encrypt certificates, and a phone that can update its system is the longer-term fix. Let's Encrypt is a free service that issues HTTPS certificates, and a phone trusts those certificates only if it recognises the Let's Encrypt root certificate. On 30 September 2021, the trust it had borrowed from another certificate authority in its early years expired, and old phones without updates, which do not recognise the newer root, began showing warnings; the explainer named the iPhone 4 and the HTC Dream as examples. Older Android versions got three more years through a special arrangement, which also ended on 30 September 2024. In 2023 Let's Encrypt advised those phones to switch to Firefox, which carries its own list that recognises the newer root. In September 2025 Mozilla announced that Firefox 144 would support only Android 8.0 and later, leaving older phones on version 143, which no longer receives updates.
