## 2026 {#y2026}

Every call you make leaves a record of both numbers, the date and the start and end times, and how long carriers keep it depends on local law; in Taiwan, rules on law enforcement queries set the retention period for mobile call records at the most recent six months. On 6 October 2020, the Grand Chamber of the Court of Justice of the European Union ruled that member states cannot require telecom providers to carry out the general and indiscriminate retention or transmission of users' traffic and location data, whether to fight crime in general or to safeguard national security. One of the cases was brought by Privacy International against the United Kingdom's surveillance laws. The judgments also said that a member state facing a serious threat to national security that is genuine and present or foreseeable can still require such retention. Call records are kept by carriers under the law, and there is no setting on your side that turns them off.
