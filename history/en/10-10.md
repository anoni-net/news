## 2026 {#y2026}

You can read Wikipedia from Taiwan, while a May 2019 statement from the Wikimedia Foundation reads that Wikipedia was blocked across all language versions in mainland China. On 10 October 2006, contributors to Chinese Wikipedia reported on the foundation's mailing list that English Wikipedia could be reached from mainland China, while the Chinese version remained blocked. The Wikipedia Signpost, the community newsletter, reported that the block began in October 2005 and that no official explanation was available for lifting it, and a November notice from Reporters Without Borders puts the unblocking of the Chinese version at around 13 November. OONI recorded 33 measurements each of Chinese and English Wikipedia from mainland China between 10 September and 10 October 2026, and all of them were anomalies (a mismatch with the control measurement, which does not by itself confirm blocking) or failures. OONI's measurements from Hong Kong over the six months to 10 October 2026 were all normal, and anyone can run OONI Probe to check which sites their own network blocks, though OONI's FAQ reads that running it can potentially be risky depending on the country.
