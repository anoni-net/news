## 2026 {#y2026}

If you browse with Safari on an iPhone, network operators can still see which site you are visiting; in August 2026 a developer reported on Apple's developer forums that Safari still sends the site name in plain text. The padlock in the address bar only means the page content is encrypted, and hiding the site name sent at the start of a connection takes another technology, Encrypted Client Hello (ECH), which Cloudflare made available on all of its plans on 29 September 2023. As of 1 October 2026, sites on Cloudflare's free plan have it on by default, and Chrome uses it by default on desktop and Android. China blocked an earlier version of ECH in July 2020, according to researchers from Geneva, GFW Report and iYouPort, and Russia's telecom regulator has blocked these connections since November 2024. To see which of these protections your browser covers, open Cloudflare's [Browser Check](https://www.cloudflare.com/ssl/encrypted-sni/) page.
