## 2026 {#y2026}

On 29 September 2023, Cloudflare announced that Encrypted Client Hello (ECH) was available on all of its plans. HTTPS encrypts the content of a page, but the TLS handshake at the start of a connection still sends the name of the site in plain text (SNI), so network operators and censorship equipment can see which site a user is visiting. ECH encrypts that part as well, using a public key published by the server, and the announcement noted that Chrome and Firefox were only starting to support it. In October 2024 Cloudflare turned ECH on by default for free plans. From November that year, Russia blocked connections that used ECH, which Risky Biz News reported as a measure by the telecom regulator Roskomnadzor. ECH became a formal standard, RFC 9849, in March 2026, and Cloudflare's documentation, updated in April, still lists it as on by default for free zones.
