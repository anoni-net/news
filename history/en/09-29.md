## 2026 {#y2026}

On 29 September 2023, Cloudflare announced that Encrypted Client Hello (ECH) was available on all of its plans. The padlock in the address bar means the page content is encrypted, but the start of a connection still sends the site name in plain text, so network operators and censorship equipment can tell which site you are visiting. ECH encrypts that part too, as long as both the site and the browser support it. Chrome has turned it on by default on desktop and Android since version 117, and Firefox has offered it since version 119. China had already begun blocking ESNI, the earlier form of ECH, in July 2020, according to researchers from Geneva, GFW Report and iYouPort, who found that the newer ECH extension was not blocked at the time. After Cloudflare made ECH the default on free plans in October 2024, Russia began blocking connections that used it from November. ECH became a formal standard, RFC 9849, in March 2026, and as of 1 October Cloudflare's documentation still lists it as on by default for free zones.
