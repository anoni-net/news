## 2026 {#y2026}

Facebook on your phone does not ask for your password every time, because it keeps you logged in with a stored credential that Facebook's announcement compared to a digital key. On 28 September 2018, Facebook disclosed a bug in "View As", a feature for previewing how your profile looks to others, which let attackers steal these credentials and take over accounts without a password. Facebook estimated that day that about 50 million accounts were affected, and made about 90 million people log back in. In an October update Facebook revised the figure to about 30 million people whose credentials were actually stolen, and the Philippine newspaper BusinessWorld reported that 755,973 of the affected accounts were in the Philippines. To make sure no one else is logged into your account, check the list of logged-in devices in Facebook's settings, where Facebook's 2018 announcement also recommended logging out of every device at once.
