## 2026 {#y2026}

Your Android phone's Privacy Dashboard shows which apps used the microphone, camera and location in the past 24 hours or 7 days. On 4 October 2021, Android 12 was released to the Android Open Source Project, and Google's developer blog said the new dashboard gives users better visibility over when apps access the microphone, camera and location data. Two years later to the day, Android 14 added a section to the location permission dialog showing whether an app shares location data with third parties. Both cover only what apps do, not tracking code on websites, which is what our story the same day is about (see [Tracking pixels that sent doctors' appointments to social platforms](https://anoni.net/news/en/2026/10/doctoralia-health-tracking-pixels/)). To see which apps have used these permissions recently, open Settings, tap Security & privacy or Privacy, and then tap Privacy dashboard.
