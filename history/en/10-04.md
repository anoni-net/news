## 2026 {#y2026}

If your phone runs Android 14 or later, the dialog that appears when an app asks for location access includes a section on whether the app shares location data with third parties. On 4 October 2023, Android 14 was released to the Android Open Source Project, and Google's developer blog said users can get more information from that section and control the app's data access. Android 12, released on the same date two years earlier, added the Privacy Dashboard, which shows which apps used the microphone, camera and location. The prompt covers only location shared by apps, not tracking code on websites, which is what our story the same day is about (see [Tracking pixels that sent doctors' appointments to social platforms](https://anoni.net/news/en/2026/10/doctoralia-health-tracking-pixels/)). To see which apps have used these permissions recently, open Settings, tap Security & privacy or Privacy, and then tap Privacy dashboard.
