---
title: Looking Back
description: One event a day from the history of privacy, anonymity networks and censorship, with a new look back added on the same date every year.
---

Looking Back revisits one event from the history of privacy, anonymity networks and censorship that happened on the same date in an earlier year. Every year, on that date, we add a new paragraph to the page, written from where we stand that year, and leave the earlier paragraphs as they were.

We check facts with what we can find at the time of writing. We do not aim for exhaustive research, and anything we learn later goes into the next year's paragraph. Over the years, each page builds up a stack of look-backs, showing how far an event sits from the present and how our own reading of it changed.
