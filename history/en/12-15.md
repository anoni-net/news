## 2026 {#y2026}

According to the judgment of West Germany's Federal Constitutional Court, the 1983 census was to use about 600,000 enumerators and ask people not only for their address but also for their workplace, how they mainly travelled to work and how long the journey took. According to a retrospective by Germany's Federal Agency for Civic Education, criticism centred on plans to cross-check the answers against residence registers and to analyse and store them by computer for the first time. Boycott slogans included "Meine Daten gehören mir" ("My data belongs to me"). Many citizens filed constitutional complaints (direct appeals to the court over rights violations), arguing that the census infringed their fundamental rights, and the court suspended it two weeks before the planned date of 27 April. In its judgment of 15 December, the court held that individuals are in principle entitled to decide for themselves on the disclosure and use of their personal data, and that the census questions themselves were constitutional while the provisions for passing the data to administrative authorities and cross-checking it against residence registers were not.

The court also required further safeguards, such as informing respondents, deleting identifying details like names and addresses as early as possible, and keeping enumerators out of their own neighbourhoods so that neighbours would not be reluctant to answer. West Germany held the census in May 1987 after adjusting its survey procedures, according to the same retrospective. As of 21 November 2026, Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act lets people ask any government agency or company holding their data to show it to them, give them copies, correct or delete it, or stop collecting and using it. In mainland China, the Personal Information Protection Law lets individuals view and copy their personal information held by those that process it, with exceptions set out in the law. They can also request deletion in listed cases, for example when the purpose of processing has been achieved.
