## 2026 {#y2026}

If you reuse a password, or the same security questions for account recovery, across different sites, a leak at any one of them puts the others at risk too. On 3 October 2017, Yahoo announced that a breach in August 2013 had affected all of its user accounts. Taiwan's Central News Agency reported the next day that this came to about 3 billion, three times the earlier estimate. Yahoo's FAQ says the stolen information may have included names, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, hashed passwords and, in some accounts, security questions and answers. Names, phone numbers and birthdays are also what doxxers piece together, and our story the same day covers how to guard against that (see [Doxxing prevention and incident response](https://anoni.net/news/en/2026/10/eff-doxxing-safety/)). Yahoo advised anyone who had used the same or similar passwords and security questions on other accounts to change them as well.
