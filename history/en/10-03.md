## 2026 {#y2026}

If you use Discord, your account has been sorted automatically into an age group since late September 2026. Discord's blog says more than 90% of users will not be asked to confirm their age, and those who are can use methods that need no ID or selfie, such as a credit card; it also notes that Google Wallet currently supports passports from only a few places, Taiwan and Singapore among them. On 3 October 2025, Discord disclosed that a third-party customer service vendor had been breached, exposing names, email addresses, IP addresses and support messages, and a later update said about 70,000 users may have had government ID photos exposed, which they had uploaded to appeal age decisions. Names, IP addresses and ID photos are exactly the material doxxers use, and our story the same day covers what to do before and after (see [Doxxing prevention and incident response](https://anoni.net/news/en/2026/10/eff-doxxing-safety/)). Discord said it would not contact anyone about the incident by phone and that official notices come only from `noreply@discord.com`, and you can check your age group under User Settings > Account Status.
