## 2026 {#y2026}

If your Apple Account's country or region is set to mainland China, iCloud is operated by GCBD (AIPO Cloud (Guizhou) Technology Co. Ltd), which Apple's help page says allows it to comply with Chinese regulations. On 7 October 1999, China's State Council issued the Regulations on the Administration of Commercial Cryptography, effective the same day. As quoted by the law firm Zhong Lun, Article 14 of that version allowed only commercial cryptography products approved by the state cryptography authority. It barred organisations and individuals from using products they had developed themselves or that were made abroad. The revised regulations of 2023 rewrote this regime and state that commercial cryptography used in mass-market consumer products is not subject to import licensing or export controls. Apple's help page says that people who are not Chinese citizens residing in mainland China can change their Apple Account's country or region to where they currently live.
