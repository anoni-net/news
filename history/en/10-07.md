## 2026 {#y2026}

If a photo editor, phone utility or VPN app that promises access to blocked websites asks you to log in with Facebook before you can use it, the login screen may be fake. On 7 October 2022, Meta said it had found more than 400 malicious Android and iOS apps that year that steal the username and password people type in. The disguises Meta listed included photo editors, phone utilities, games, business tools and VPNs claiming to boost browsing speed or grant access to blocked content or websites. Meta's announcement advised checking an app's download count, ratings and reviews, including negative ones, before installing it, and being wary of apps that do nothing until you log in. If you have already logged in through such an app, Meta recommends deleting it right away, resetting your password and turning on two-factor authentication, preferably with an authenticator app.
