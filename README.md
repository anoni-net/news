# anoni.net 新聞導讀

**正體中文** | [English](#en)

`anoni.net/news` 的文章與網站原始碼。整理國際上隱私、匿名網路與網路審查的新聞，一則寫成一篇導讀。我們從科技與開源生態的角度讀新聞，介紹背後有哪些專案與技術，可以成為保護隱私的解決方案，或讓需要它們的人把工作做得更有效率。每篇附原文連結、來源與日期、用自己的話寫的摘要，以及導讀觀點，篇幅控制在幾分鐘讀得完。

由[匿名網路社群 anoni.net](https://anoni.net/) 維護，是 anoni.net 底下的產品之一。

## 選題

主軸是隱私、匿名網路與網路審查。在這個範圍裡，優先選能帶出具體專案或技術的新聞，例如開源工具的新發布或重大功能、附上方法與程式碼的研究、開放資料集與 API、數位安全的工具與流程。其次是會讓既有做法需要調整的變化，例如常用工具改變使用方式、加密與匿名工具面臨的政策變動、新的監控手法與檢測方式。

跟隱私、匿名與審查無關的科技新聞、版本公告、沒有技術面可談的政策聲明與閉源產品的行銷文不選。主要用途是追查特定個人的工具也不選，導讀容易成為濫用的操作說明。

工具類的文章寫稿前先看過專案頁面，確認授權、最近一次發布的時間、使用門檻與介面語言。最近一年沒有發布的專案不選，除非文章本身就在說明它停止維護。導讀寫工具能做什麼、誰在維護、如何取得，不寫哪個地區、哪個族群的人在用它。

## 導讀歷史

導讀的支線專欄，每天一則，回看隱私、匿名網路與網路審查在同一天發生過的事。每年到了同一天加一則新的快照，舊的保留原樣，查證以寫稿當下查得到的資料為準。格式見 [`SPEC.md`](./SPEC.md) 的「導讀歷史」，寫法見 [`guides/history.md`](./guides/history.md)。

## 語系

每篇都有正體中文、簡體中文與英文三個版本，同時發布。三個版本的事實相同，導讀觀點依各自的讀者重寫，英文版多寫同一件事在亞洲各地的情況。

## 跟文件站的分工

| 內容 | 放在哪裡 |
|---|---|
| 新聞導讀（摘要與評註） | 本 repo |
| 外部文章的全文翻譯 | 文件站的 blog |
| 軟體更新日誌 | 文件站的 `changelog/` |
| 社群公告與活動 | 文件站的 blog |

導讀只寫摘要與評註，不整篇翻譯。原文的著作權屬於原作者，每一則都附上原文連結。

## 狀態

籌備中。網站由本 repo 的 `build.py` 產生，不採用現成的靜態網站產生器，理由、內容格式、網址規則與第一版不做的功能寫在 [`SPEC.md`](./SPEC.md)。開發指令與寫稿步驟在 [`AGENTS.md`](./AGENTS.md)，寫作指南在 [`guides/`](./guides/)。

## 寫作規則

照社群首頁的[寫作風格規範](https://anoni.net/join/writing-style/)，本 repo 不另外寫一份。給 AI 協作工具的說明在 [`AGENTS.md`](./AGENTS.md)。

文章內容以 [CC-BY 4.0](./LICENSE) 授權。之後加入的程式碼另外標示授權。

# anoni.net News

[正體中文](#zh-tw) | **English**

Articles and site source for `anoni.net/news`. We gather international news on privacy, anonymity networks and internet censorship and write each item up as a short briefing. We read the news from a technology and open-source angle, introducing the projects and tools behind it that can protect privacy or help the people who need them work more effectively. Each briefing links to the original, gives its source and date, summarises it in our own words and adds our perspective, and takes a few minutes to read.

It is maintained by [anoni.net](https://anoni.net/), a community based in Taiwan, as one of the projects under anoni.net.

## Story selection

The focus is privacy, anonymity networks and internet censorship. Within that scope we prefer news that leads to a concrete project or technology: a new release or major feature of an open-source tool, research published with its methods and code, open datasets and APIs, and digital security tools and practices. Next come changes that mean existing practice has to adapt, such as a widely used tool changing how it works, policy shifts affecting encryption and anonymity tools, and new surveillance techniques and ways to detect them.

We skip technology news unrelated to privacy, anonymity or censorship, release announcements, policy statements with no technical side to discuss, and marketing for closed-source products. We also skip tools whose main use is tracking down particular individuals, since a briefing on one could easily serve as a how-to for abuse.

Before writing about a tool we read its project page and check its license, the date of its latest release, how hard it is to use and which languages its interface supports. A project with no release in the past year is left out, unless the article is about it being unmaintained. A briefing covers what a tool does, who maintains it and how to get it, and never which region or group of people uses it.

## On this day

A side column of the briefings, one entry a day, looking back at what happened on the same date in privacy, anonymity networks and internet censorship. Each year a new snapshot is added on the same date and the older ones stay as they were, fact-checked against what could be verified when they were written. The format is under "導讀歷史" in [`SPEC.md`](./SPEC.md) and the writing guide is [`guides/history.md`](./guides/history.md) (both in Chinese).

## Languages

Every briefing is published in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and English at the same time. The facts are the same in all three, and the perspective is rewritten for each audience, with the English version adding how the same story plays out across Asia.

## Division of work with the docs site

| Content | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| News briefings (summaries and commentary) | This repository |
| Full translations of outside articles | The docs site's blog |
| Software changelogs | The docs site's `changelog/` |
| Community announcements and events | The docs site's blog |

Briefings contain summaries and commentary only, never full translations. Copyright in the original belongs to its author, and every briefing links to it.

## Status

In preparation. The site is generated by `build.py` in this repository rather than an off-the-shelf static site generator. The reasons, content format, URL rules and the features left out of the first version are in [`SPEC.md`](./SPEC.md). Development commands and the steps for writing a briefing are in [`AGENTS.md`](./AGENTS.md), and the writing guides are in [`guides/`](./guides/). These files are in Chinese.

## Writing style

We follow the community's [writing style guide](https://anoni.net/en/join/writing-style/) rather than keeping a separate copy here. Notes for AI coding assistants are in [`AGENTS.md`](./AGENTS.md).
