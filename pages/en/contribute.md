---
title: Write a column
description: anoni.net News welcomes column submissions on privacy, anonymity networks, internet censorship and the open source ecosystem behind them. This page covers suitable topics, how to write, bylines and how to submit.
---

Columns are the signed pieces in anoni.net News where an author sets out their own view. Briefings gather what each side says without judging who is right; a column makes an argument, and the views belong to the author, not to the anoni.net community. We welcome submissions from researchers, project maintainers, people working in the field and readers who care about these issues.

## Suitable topics {#topics}

The focus is the same as the briefings: privacy, anonymity networks and internet censorship, along with the open source ecosystem these tools depend on. A column fits when several news stories point to the same change and you have a view on that change, such as the effects you have seen in your work or as a user after a policy or platform rule changed, or a trade-off faced by a project you help maintain.

A piece that only retells one story, without an argument of its own, is better suited to a briefing. As with the briefings, we do not take pieces whose main use is tracking down particular individuals or instructions that could be abused.

## How to write {#writing}

Build the piece around one argument and state it in the first two paragraphs. Mark your own views in the first person and link to the source for each fact, so readers can tell which sentences are facts and which are your judgement. When you discuss views that differ from yours, quote the other side's own documents and answer their strongest reasons.

There is no length limit. Keep paragraphs to two to four sentences and write in full paragraphs rather than bullet points. **Save bold for the points you most want to get across**; the site marks them with a highlighter in the column colour. You may end with one or two questions for readers.

You can write in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese or English. Maintainers translate the other two versions and will ask you to check them before publication. Wording follows the community's [writing style guide](https://anoni.net/en/join/writing-style/), and maintainers will help adjust it during review. The detailed rules are in the [column guide](https://github.com/anoni-net/news/blob/main/guides/column.md) on GitHub (in Chinese).

## Boundaries {#boundaries}

A column is your opinion, but the same boundaries as the briefings apply:

- Keep names of people and organisations only where they are needed to understand the story, and do not name people who have been singled out or attacked
- Do not describe which tools people in a particular region or group use, since a region, a group and a product name together are enough to serve as an investigative lead
- Do not translate other people's articles in full, and keep quotations short

## Bylines {#byline}

Every column carries a byline: your real name or a pen name you use consistently, never the community's name. A column under the community byline would read as the community's position.

A pen name ties together everything one person writes, and the more you write, the easier it becomes to trace the name back to you from your topics, timing and style. Anyone who needs strong anonymity should take this into account.

## How to submit {#submit}

If you do not want to leave any trace of your identity, ask a maintainer to submit on your behalf. Send your draft by direct message in the community's [Matrix room](https://matrix.to/#/#community:im.anoni.net), by email to `whisper@anoni.net` (PGP encryption recommended), or through the [sensitive upload process](https://anoni.net/en/join/upload-sensitive/). Maintainers submit under their own identity, and you will not appear in any record.

If you are comfortable with GitHub, you can open a pull request on [anoni-net/news](https://github.com/anoni-net/news) yourself. This leaves your GitHub account, the commit author, email address and time zone on record, so it suits people writing under their real name.

## Review and publication {#review}

Maintainers check facts, sources, wording and the boundaries above, and do not change your argument. Please accept changes on facts and boundaries; the wording and the argument are yours to decide. Once reviewed, the column is scheduled into a publishing slot and goes live in all three languages at once, licensed under CC BY 4.0 like the other briefings.
