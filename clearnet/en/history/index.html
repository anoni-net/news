Same Day, Years Apart

One event a day from the history of privacy, anonymity networks and censorship, with a new look back added on the same date every year.

Also in 正體中文, 简体中文

After each day's stories, we look back at something that happened on the same date in an earlier year and follow it to where you stand today. On 29 September 2023, for example, Cloudflare let websites encrypt the site name that a connection reveals, and that entry covers whether your browser uses it now and where it has been blocked.

Every year on the same date, we add a new paragraph to the page and keep the earlier ones as they were, each marked with the year it was written. Over time, a page shows how far an event sits from the present and how our own reading of it changed. We check facts with what we can find at the time of writing, put anything we learn later into the next year's paragraph, and mark corrections with a date when we get something wrong.
