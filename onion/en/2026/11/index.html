Tighter limits on outside contributions to open source projects in the AI era

curl ended its bug bounty, and GitHub now lets maintainers limit who can open pull requests and issues; in 2026 open source projects are narrowing the ways outsiders can contribute. The author argues that this addresses the volume problem created by floods of low-quality submissions, but the trust problem remains. The malicious releases in both the xz backdoor and the Shai-Hulud worm were published from developer accounts that were already trusted, and what is missing is review capacity and permission management.
